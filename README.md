## About the Essay

This course serves as an introduction to the theory and practice that make reducing costs and improving the quality of manufactured products possible in the 21st century.

Participants will learn the principles that govern all processes, how to distiguish signals from noise, and how to make better data-driven decisions

Topics include:

- The economics of manufacturing
- The two types of variation: common cause and assignable cause
- The role of natural process limits and how to calculate them
- How to characterize process behavior
- How to construct an XmR chart

If you've ever wondered why comparisons between numbers rarely lead to process improvement, curious about what it means to understand variation, or you're simply looking for a new perspective, this course is for you!
